\section{Introduction}

WolfBite is a Flutter/Firebase shopping assistant for WIC participants. In
Project 1a, the team reverse engineered its behavior and independently tested
its principal user flows. This report uses market evidence and independent LLM
analysis to decide whether to extend WolfBite or pivot, then defines a product
that four students can build and test in one month.

\subsection{Method}

against direct sources. A rival was retained only when at least two models
named it or one model supplied a live, relevant URL. The team separately
checked whether each retained product was real, addressed a comparable
audience or problem, and supported the attributed capabilities.
